## the audit

Pick up anything near you. Genuinely, anything.

Somebody designed it. Somebody else specified the material, and somebody
extracted that material out of the ground in a country you probably could not
place on a map under time pressure. Somebody ran the machine that formed it.
Somebody inspected it and had a bad day and inspected it anyway. Somebody boxed
it. Somebody drove it to a port at four in the morning. Somebody crossed an
ocean with it. Somebody unloaded it. Somebody stocked the shelf.

Not one of those people knows you exist. Every one of them had to not fail for
that object to be in your hand.

<Ledger kind="fact">
You are held up, continuously, by an enormous number of strangers, none of whom
you have verified.
</Ledger>

<Ledger kind="feeling">
Once you actually see it, it is difficult to look at a room the same way.
</Ledger>

<Ledger kind="interpretation">
I do not think this proves God. I want to be clear about that, because it is a
tempting move and it is not a valid one.
</Ledger>

<Ledger kind="later">
What it did was remove my excuse. I had been saying I was not a person of faith,
and it turned out I was doing it constantly and had simply never priced it. The
question was never whether I had faith. It was only ever where I was putting it.
</Ledger>
