Then there was somebody else.

I am going to be extremely careful in this chapter, and the care is not
politeness. It is accuracy. Almost everything I *felt* during this period was
built on almost nothing that I *knew*, and that gap is the entire lesson.

He gets no name and no description. He is <Redacted as="another man" who="a person I do not know and will not characterise" /> and
nothing more. He has done nothing to me. I want that stated plainly at the top,
because what follows is not fair to him and I know it.

## the evidence, in full

<Break>
  <Monument size="huge">
    Here is everything<br />
    I <em className="italic">actually saw.</em>
  </Monument>
</Break>

<SplitPerception />

Five things. Two people sitting close, laughing, talking easily, comfort.

That is the complete evidentiary record. I did not see anything else. I was not
told anything else by anyone who would know.

## the story I built out of it

From those five observations my mind constructed a full narrative with
motivation, timeline, and a moral position for me to occupy.

<Ledger kind="memory">
I saw two people sitting close. I had heard, secondhand, that he had liked her
for a long time.
</Ledger>

<Ledger kind="feeling">
Replaced. Cheated. Forgotten. Stupid for still caring. Afraid that I had
misread the entire thing from the beginning.
</Ledger>

<Ledger kind="interpretation">
So it was never what I thought it was. So the psalms night meant nothing. So I
had been managed.
</Ledger>

<Ledger kind="anger">
you played me. you said forever. i was reading scripture to you at four in the
morning and this is where it goes.
</Ledger>

<Ledger kind="later">
Every one of those was a feeling wearing the grammar of a fact. Read them again
and notice that not one of them is a thing I observed. They are all conclusions,
and the evidence they rest on is five items long.
</Ledger>

I wrote things during this period that I have kept and will not clean up. Some
of it is angry and some of it accuses someone of things I have no basis to
accuse anyone of. It stays in the archive as what it is — writing done in pain,
which is a real category, and not a category that gets to be evidence.

## the part that was actually mine

Here is what I eventually had to admit, and it took a while.

I had said *I let you go.* I had said it out loud, more than once, and I had
believed I meant it.

But my reaction to seeing her laugh near somebody else was not the reaction of a
man who had let go. It was the reaction of a man who had filed a claim and
expected it to be honoured.

Which means I had not released anything. I had loaned it.

<Ledger kind="question">
Did I want her to be happy, or did I want her to be happy in a way that still
had me in it?
</Ledger>

That question is chapter nineteen, and I did not have an answer for it for a
long time.
