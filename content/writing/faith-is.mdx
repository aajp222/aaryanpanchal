faith is the car starting.
faith is your eyes opening.
faith is the floor.
faith is gravity, and not checking.

faith is the stranger who built the thing,
the stranger who packed the thing,
the stranger who carried the thing
across an ocean, in the dark,
while you slept.

faith is.
not just in prayer.
