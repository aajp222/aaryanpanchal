people act like faith is a church thing. its not. you have faith every
morning and you dont even clock it. you turn the key and you believe the
car starts. you open your eyes and you believe the floor is still there.
you take a pill made by someone you will never meet, packed by someone
else you will never meet, flown across an ocean by a third person, and
you swallow it without blinking. thats faith. thats thousands of
strangers you are trusting with your actual life before breakfast. so
dont tell me you dont have any. you just picked where to put it.
